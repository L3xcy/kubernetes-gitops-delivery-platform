1. Verify the workload using the project-owned kubeconfig:

  kubectl --kubeconfig .local/kubeconfig --context kind-gitops-delivery \
    -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} get pods

2. Access the service locally:

  kubectl --kubeconfig .local/kubeconfig --context kind-gitops-delivery \
    -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward service/{{ include "delivery-demo.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
